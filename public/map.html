PineconeCourse lessons mapped to real-life situations

Website concept

From lessons to the questions people ask

The course has 17 lessons in 5 modules. People don't arrive asking for a lesson. They arrive in a situation, with a question. Tap any lesson or situation to trace the connection.

Possible questions for each situation

Written the way people type them into Google or an AI assistant, so each one can become a page title. Dots show which modules answer it.

What the map shows

  • Situations cut across modules."Starting my first job" pulls from Modules 1 and 4. "Moving out" pulls from Modules 2 and 3. A situation page gathers pieces of several lessons.
  • A few lessons carry a lot.Managing Credit and Workplace Investing each feed several situations, so they are the best first pages to build.
  • Concept lessons work as engines.Compound interest, present value and inflation are rarely searched by name. They power the answers inside other questions.
Mapped from lesson and part titles in the live course (Revised Modules 1 to 3, Modules 4 and 5). Questions are proposals to check against search data and user sessions.
