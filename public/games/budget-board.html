How do I make a budget I'll actually stick to?

Build it from the pieces of your life. Tap a piece, or drag it into a column, then type the amount. Add your own pieces too. The balance updates as you go.

Before you start, ask yourself
  • What do I want this budget to do for me?
  • Which costs stay the same every month, and which ones move?
  • What amount could I set aside every month, before anything else?
