How do I start making a budget?

Fill in what comes in, what you set aside for yourself, and what goes out each month. The totals update as you type.

Before you start, ask yourself
  1. What do I want this budget to do for me?
  2. Which costs stay the same every month, and which ones move?
  3. What amount could I set aside every month, before anything else?
