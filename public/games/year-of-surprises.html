      So, what's your number?

      Many people aim for 3 to 6 months of spending. That's a starting point. These three questions help you pick yours.

      • How steady is your income?If it goes up and down, aim higher.
      • What's your most likely surprise?An old car, a pet, a health bill. Think about what it would cost.
      • If your pay stopped, how long until the next job?That's how many months you'd need to cover.
